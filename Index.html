HHHHHMM Quality of Life Scale

Assesses geriatric or chronically ill dogs across 7 key parameters (Score 0–10 each).

49 / 70 Total Points
1. HURT (Pain Control & Breathing) 7 / 10

Is pain controlled? Breathing adequate and painless?

2. HUNGER (Eating Willingness) 7 / 10

Is the dog eating willingly? Hand feeding required?

3. HYDRATION (Fluid Balance) 7 / 10

Is skin turgor normal? Are fluids required?

4. HYGIENE (Cleanliness & Sores) 7 / 10

Can dog be kept clean/dry? Pressure sores present?

5. HAPPINESS (Joy & Engagement) 7 / 10

Shows joy, greets family, wags tail, plays with toys?

6. MOBILITY (Standing & Walking) 7 / 10

Can dog rise without assistance? Walk comfortably?

7. MORE GOOD DAYS THAN BAD 7 / 10

Do good days outnumber bad days? Is human-animal bond intact?

Acceptable Quality of Life (> 35 Points)

A total score above 35 indicates that the dog's quality of life remains acceptable under current management. Continue monitoring weekly.
