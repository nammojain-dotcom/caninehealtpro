The Rib Check

Run your thumbs along the spine and fingers over the ribs. Ribs should feel like the back of your hand—palpable without excess fat cover.

The Overhead View

Look down from above while your dog is standing. Look for a clear waist indentation ("hourglass" shape) behind the ribcage.

The Profile Tuck

Look from the side at eye level. The abdomen should slope gracefully upward (abdominal tuck) behind the ribcage toward the rear legs.

The Oral-Systemic Health Link

Why oral hygiene directly impacts cardiac and renal longevity

Periodontal disease is the most widespread clinical condition in adult dogs. Pathogenic oral bacteria form plaque biofilm, leading to gingival inflammation and loss of periodontal ligament attachments.

Systemic Dissemination: Chronic inflammation allows oral bacteria to enter the bloodstream (bacteremia), placing recurring bacterial micro-emboli on cardiac valves (causing endocarditis) and renal filtration nephrons (accelerating chronic kidney disease).

Home Maintenance Standard Brush teeth 3x+ per week using enzymatic pet toothpaste. Never use human toothpaste (fluoride & foaming agents are toxic).
Professional Clinical COHAT Schedule a professional dental cleaning under anesthesia every 12–24 months to clean subgingival plaque where true disease resides.
